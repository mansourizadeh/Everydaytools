How to use the unit converter

  1. Select a category such as Length or Temperature.
  2. Enter a value and choose the From and To units.
  3. Copy the result or swap the units.

Example: 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm, 1 pound is 0.45359237 kg, and 100 °F is about 37.78 °C using °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9. So 212 °F is 100 °C.

Which gallon does the tool use?

The US gallon, which is 3.785411784 liters.
