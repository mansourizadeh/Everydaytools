Currency Converter

Convert between major currencies using rates you enter and save in your browser. This tool does not show live market prices, which keeps it private, free and usable offline.

How to use the currency converter

  1. Open Edit exchange rates, enter current rates per 1 USD from a source you trust, and save.
  2. Enter an amount and choose the currencies.
  3. Use the swap button to reverse the conversion.

Example: if you set 1 USD = 0.92 EUR, then 100 USD converts to 92 EUR. The tool shows the date your rates were last saved.

Frequently asked questions

Are the exchange rates live?

No. Rates are entered manually and may differ from live market or bank rates. Always check a trusted source before making financial decisions.

Where are my rates stored?

Only in the local storage of your own browser.
