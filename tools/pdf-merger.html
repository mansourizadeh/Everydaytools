PDF Merger

Join several PDFs into a single file. Add your documents, arrange them in order and download the merged PDF. Files are processed locally and never uploaded.

How to use the pdf merger

  1. Add two or more PDF files (up to 50 MB each).
  2. Use the arrows to reorder files or remove any you do not need.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and download merged.pdf.

Example: combine a cover letter, CV and certificates into one application PDF in the right order.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to merge confidential PDFs here?

Yes. Merging runs inside your browser and your files are not sent to any server.

Why did one of my files fail?

Password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password first, then try again.
