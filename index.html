About

EverydayTools is a small collection of free utilities for common tasks. There is no account, no ads tracking and no server processing: calculators, QR codes, image resizing and PDF merging all run inside your browser.

Privacy

Your images, PDFs and inputs are never uploaded. Only harmless preferences (theme, recently used tools and exchange rates you type yourself) are kept in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them any time from your browser settings. The PDF merger and QR generator load an open-source library from a public CDN when first opened; no personal data is sent with that request.
